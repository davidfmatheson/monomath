About Jeannine

An advocate who shows up for her community

Jeannine Casselman is a public interest attorney who has dedicated her career to making the legal system work for people who have historically been shut out of it. She represents individuals and families facing housing instability, unsafe living conditions, and barriers to the benefits and care they need.

Her work is grounded in the belief that legal problems are rarely just legal problems. An eviction affects a child’s schooling; a moldy apartment affects a family’s health. That is why she partners closely with health care providers, community organizations, and advocates to address the root causes of the challenges her clients face.

A proud member of the Boston community, Jeannine brings both professional skill and personal commitment to every matter, and treats every client with dignity, patience, and respect.
