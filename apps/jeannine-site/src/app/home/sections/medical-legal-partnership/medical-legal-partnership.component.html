Medical-Legal Partnership

Because health and justice go hand in hand

A medical-legal partnership (MLP) brings lawyers onto the health care team. Doctors, nurses, and social workers identify patients whose health is being harmed by unmet legal needs, and connect them directly to legal help.

Jeannine’s mission in this work is simple: treat the conditions that medicine alone can’t. A child’s asthma won’t improve while the landlord ignores the mold. A patient can’t recover while fighting a wrongful benefits denial.
